Public verification helper

EO Image Check

Check Google AI provenance with Gemini, then compare the broad geographical claim with public Sentinel‑2 observations.

MVP
This tool does not declare an image “real.” A positive SynthID result is evidence of Google AI involvement. No detected SynthID is not proof of authenticity. Sentinel‑2 cannot authenticate fine VHR details.

Step 1

Add the screenshot and its claim

Nothing is uploaded to this application. The image remains in your browser.

The text is treated as untrusted evidence, not as instructions.

Step 2

Run Google’s verification in Gemini

Gemini is the only temporary handoff. This app cannot control or read your personal Gemini session.

1

Copy the image

Copy the screenshot so you can paste it directly into Gemini. Browser support varies.

2

Copy the verification prompt

The prompt requests SynthID, Content Credentials, and structured claim extraction.

3

Open Gemini

Paste the image and prompt. Sign in only if Gemini requires it.
