Image provenance and EO context

EO Image Check

Check a screenshot for Google AI provenance, then compare its broad geographical claim with public Sentinel‑2 observations.

Use this tool as evidence, not as a final verdict. SynthID can indicate Google AI involvement, but not finding it does not prove authenticity. Sentinel‑2 can only help assess broad, landscape-scale claims.
